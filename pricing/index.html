FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes, forever. Free includes three starter programs, logging, rest timers and goal rings. No card needed.

How does the free trial work?

Pro and Team start with 14 days free. We remind you two days before it ends; cancel in Settings and you’re never charged.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Your history, PRs and streaks stay; Pro-only features simply lock until you upgrade again.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes — 40% off Pro annual with a verified student email.
