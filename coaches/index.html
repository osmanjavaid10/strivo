How the team and Ava work together

Guardrails first. Then personalisation.

  1. 01

    Coaches set the limits

    Maximum weekly load jumps, minimum recovery between hard sessions and every exercise swap are defined by humans.

  2. 02

    Ava adapts inside them

    The AI chooses within those limits from your readiness, performance and form — and always explains why.

  3. 03

    Humans review the edge cases

    Pain reports, long breaks and plateaus are flagged to the team weekly to improve the rules for everyone.
