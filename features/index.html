Features

Everything a great coach does. In your pocket.Everything a great coach does. In your pocket.

Strivo plans your training, watches every rep, answers your questions and shows you the proof — on iOS and Android, with or without a gym.

Adaptive plansReal-time form checkAI coachProgress you can see

01 — Adaptive plans

A plan that rewrites itself every night.

Tell Strivo your goal, schedule and kit once. Each evening it reads your sleep, soreness and last session, then adjusts tomorrow — load, volume, even the exercise picks.

  • Readiness score from sleep, HRV and how you rate soreness
  • Missed a day? The week reshuffles instead of piling up
  • Equipment profiles for home, gym and hotel

02 — Real-time form check

It sees the rep you can’t.

Prop your phone against a water bottle. On-device vision tracks 17 body points at 30 frames a second and gives one clear cue at a time, spoken or on screen.

  • Depth, knee track, bar path, tempo and range of motion
  • Per-rep form score so you can watch technique improve
  • Runs fully on-device — no video ever leaves your phone

03 — AI coach

Ask Coach Ava. She’s read your training log.

Questions get answers that know your plan, history and form data. “My knee feels off” changes today’s session, not just the conversation.

  • Swaps, substitutions and deloads in plain language
  • Explains the why behind every change it makes
  • Monthly 15-minute check-in with a human coach on Pro

04 — Progress you can see

Proof, not vibes.

Estimated 1RMs, volume, streaks and form scores in one honest view. Weekly rings keep you consistent; the strength curve shows why it matters.

  • Strength curves for every main lift
  • Weekly goal rings for sessions, minutes and mobility
  • Exports to Apple Health and Health Connect

And the details

Small things that make it stick.

  • Smart rest timer

    Rest stretches when bar speed drops and shortens when you’re fresh.

  • Works offline

    Download a week of sessions for basements, planes and patchy gyms.

  • Watch companion

    Log sets and see cues on Apple Watch and Wear OS.

  • Exercise swaps

    Machine taken? One tap finds an equivalent that keeps the plan intact.

  • Team challenges

    Share streaks and weekly goals with up to five people on Team.

  • Private by design

    Pose data is processed on-device. No ads, no data sales, ever.

  • Health sync

    Reads sleep and heart-rate variability to set your readiness.

  • Adaptive movements

    Seated, banded and low-impact variations for every pattern.
