Changelog

Shipped, week by week.Shipped, week by week.

Every beta release since we opened the doors. The public 1.0 lands in spring 2027.

  1. v0.9.0

    November 12, 2026

    Watch companion and offline weeks

    • NewApple Watch and Wear OS companion: log sets and see form cues on your wrist.
    • NewDownload a full week of sessions for training without signal.
    • ImprovedRest timer now adapts to bar speed between sets.

  2. v0.8.2

    October 29, 2026

    Sharper knee tracking

    • ImprovedKnee-track detection is 18% more accurate in low light.
    • FixedForm score no longer resets when the phone briefly loses focus.

  3. v0.8.0

    October 8, 2026

    Coach Ava learns your schedule

    • NewAva moves hard sessions around travel and busy days from your calendar.
    • NewPain-aware swaps designed by our physical-therapy team.
    • FixedWeekly rings now count mobility minutes logged from Apple Health.

  4. v0.7.0

    September 10, 2026

    Team plan beta

    • NewTrain with up to five people: shared challenges and team streaks.
    • ImprovedProgress view loads twice as fast on older Android phones.
