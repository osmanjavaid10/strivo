Using Strivo

You must be 16 or older. Strivo gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

Subscriptions

Pro and Team renew automatically each month or year until cancelled. Free trials last 14 days; we remind you two days before they end. Cancel from Settings and you keep access until the paid period ends.

Your content

Your training data is yours. You give us permission to process it only to run the service for you, as described in the privacy policy.

Acceptable use

Don’t reverse-engineer the app, resell access, or use Strivo to harass other members in team challenges.

Liability

Train within your limits. To the extent the law allows, Strivo isn’t liable for injuries from exercise performed while using the app.

Changes

We’ll tell you in the app at least 30 days before any change to these terms that affects you.
