The short version

Your camera frames never leave your phone. We store the numbers we derive from them — joint angles, depth and tempo — so your progress view works. We don’t sell data, show ads or use third-party trackers.

What we collect

Account details: your email address and the name you choose to show.

Training data: sessions, sets, weights, form scores and the soreness ratings you enter.

Health data you allow: sleep and heart-rate variability from Apple Health or Health Connect, used only to calculate readiness.

How we use it

To build and adapt your plan, power Coach Ava’s answers, and show your progress. Aggregated, anonymised data helps our coaching team improve the rules for everyone.

Your controls

Export everything as JSON or CSV from Settings at any time. Delete your account in two taps; your data is erased from our servers within 30 days and from backups within 90.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and sends nothing from its forms. Your theme choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage only.

Contact

Questions go to privacy@strivo.example.
