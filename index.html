How Strivo works

A coach that sees, thinks and adapts — every single session.

  1. 01 / 04

    Plan

    Tell Strivo your goal, schedule and kit. It builds four weeks of training — then rewrites tomorrow every night from your sleep, soreness and last session.

    Re-planned nightly

  2. 02 / 04

    Workout

    Every set, rep and rest is timed for you. Weights auto-progress when your bar speed says you’re ready, and back off when it says you’re not.

    Auto-progressing loads

  3. 03 / 04

    Form check

    Prop your phone up. On-device vision tracks 17 body points and gives one clear cue at a time — depth, knee track, tempo — without a single frame leaving your phone.

    100% on-device

  4. 04 / 04

    Progress

    Strength curves, streaks and form scores in one honest view. Maya added 17.5 kg to her squat in 12 weeks — and can see exactly how.

    +17.5 kg in 12 weeks

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Does form check send video to the cloud?

    No. Pose detection runs entirely on your phone. Strivo only stores the derived numbers — joint angles, depth and tempo — never images or video.

    Do I need a gym?

    No. Tell Strivo what you have — a full gym, a pair of dumbbells, or nothing — and every program adapts its exercise picks to that kit.

    Which phones are supported?

    iPhone XS or newer on iOS 17+, and Android phones from 2020 onward on Android 11+. Form check needs the rear or front camera.

    Is the AI coach a real person?

    Coach Ava is an AI trained with Strivo’s coaching team. Pro members can also book a monthly 15-minute video check-in with a human coach.

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes. Cancel in two taps from Settings; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then drop to Free with your history intact.

    When does Strivo launch?

    Spring 2027 on the App Store and Google Play. Join the waitlist for early access and three months of Pro free.
