How it works

From first tap to first PR.From first tap to first PR.

Strivo works like a coach who never misses a session: it plans, watches, adapts and reports back. Here’s what happens under the hood.

How Strivo works

A coach that sees, thinks and adapts — every single session.

  1. 01 / 04

    Plan

    Tell Strivo your goal, schedule and kit. It builds four weeks of training — then rewrites tomorrow every night from your sleep, soreness and last session.

    Re-planned nightly

  2. 02 / 04

    Workout

    Every set, rep and rest is timed for you. Weights auto-progress when your bar speed says you’re ready, and back off when it says you’re not.

    Auto-progressing loads

  3. 03 / 04

    Form check

    Prop your phone up. On-device vision tracks 17 body points and gives one clear cue at a time — depth, knee track, tempo — without a single frame leaving your phone.

    100% on-device

  4. 04 / 04

    Progress

    Strength curves, streaks and form scores in one honest view. Maya added 17.5 kg to her squat in 12 weeks — and can see exactly how.

    +17.5 kg in 12 weeks

Form check, under the hood

Every rep, measured in four steps. All on your phone.

17 pts · 30 fps · on-device

  1. 01

    Capture

    The camera streams frames at 30 fps into memory. Nothing is recorded or saved.

  2. 02

    Track

    A compact pose model finds 17 body points per frame on the phone’s neural engine.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Joint angles, depth, bar path and tempo are computed per rep and scored 0–100.

  4. 04

    Cue

    The single most important fix is spoken or shown — then the frame is discarded.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Strivo decide what I do tomorrow?

It combines your plan’s weekly targets with readiness (sleep, HRV and your soreness rating) and last session’s performance, then adjusts load, volume or exercise choice within safe limits set by our coaching team.

Where do I put my phone for form check?

About two metres away at hip height: side-on for squats and deadlifts, front-on for lunges. A live outline shows when you’re fully in frame.

What if I train without my phone?

Log the session afterwards in under a minute. Strivo still adapts — it just won’t have form data for those reps.

Does form check send video to the cloud?

No. Pose detection runs entirely on your phone. Strivo only stores the derived numbers — joint angles, depth and tempo — never images or video.
